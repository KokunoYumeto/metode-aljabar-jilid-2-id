# O014 course scope and bounded comparison

Decision: use Wen-Wei Li Volume 2 as the integrated O014/D80 spine.

## Integrated course shape

Prerequisites are groups, rings, modules, fields, and category theory. Volume
1/O013 supplies them; prior experience with complexes and cohomology is useful
but not logically required. The author targets advanced undergraduates,
graduate students, researchers, instructors, and self-learners.

The coherent core is the Prelude plus Chapters 1-5:

1. category-theory review;
2. abelian categories;
3. complexes, resolutions, and classical derived functors;
4. triangulated and derived categories;
5. spectral sequences.

Chapters 6-9 and Appendices A-B remain source-order extensions: group
(co)homology, monads/Morita/descent, simplicial methods/Dold-Kan, duality and
Tannakian reconstruction, further abelian-category results, and ind/pro
objects. The author says the entire 650-page volume exceeds one academic year;
the inner part can be compressed into one semester only in reduced mode.

Two bridges must be authored rather than attributed to Li: worked
diagram-chasing/solution support, and an accessible semantic/HTML surface.

## Bounded alternative

The only alternative evaluated was Tom Leinster's *Basic Category Theory*
plus the Stacks Project's *Homological Algebra* and *Derived Categories*.

- Leinster arXiv v1 source archive: 215,649 bytes, SHA-256
  `b5bb37452d574423bd842865cd988b1e0c661f541138456af9eb40228bc7e7af`.
- Leinster PDF: 191 pages, 1,322,462 bytes, SHA-256
  `b908b00abacf5907b6f9c497bf84786d7cc0fd8b47f3c6797e8d7ac0f9fece01`.
- Leinster: 107 exercises, 9 explicit hints, no solutions; CC BY-NC-SA
  4.0, with an unlicensed Cambridge class file that should be replaced.
- Stacks commit: `ed88ff783bcb4dd9a28518a33b028841094009cf`.
- Stacks *Homological Algebra*: 82 pages, SHA-256
  `f3d73df1c27a6d88f9e4dd0f01697b3dd8553b88e3ea078539a9b512dc4d9576`.
- Stacks *Derived Categories*: 129 pages, SHA-256
  `3d6c5169149c3a8556cab5bbfc5ce27ca34607fb1d5da49456277f4410ddcae3`.
- Stacks modules are GFDL 1.2-or-later and contain no formal exercise bank.

The 402-page alternative spans the topic names but is not a coherent teaching
course: Leinster stops before abelian/homological algebra; the Stacks modules
are terse references with broad cross-project dependencies and no aligned
mastery layer. It also creates a CC BY-NC-SA/GFDL component-separation burden.

Leinster remains an optional gentle donor and terminology comparator. Stacks'
stable semantic HTML is useful as a verification/accessibility reference. They
do not replace Li's single-author progression, notation, license, and direct
continuity with O013.
